## Overview

ConfConf 2025 happened in Sofia, Bulgaria on July 6-7, 2025. We brought together organizers from 23 organizations to share their experiences and learn from each other about running FOSS conferences.

The unconference format really worked. People organized their own sessions, we did a lot of brainstorming, and there was plenty of time for conversations between talks. We talked about how to build community at events, managing teams, picking speakers, dealing with infrastructure, and getting people to participate spontaneously.

## What worked

**Format:** The unconference approach let things happen naturally. Short sessions worked better than long ones.

**Diversity:** We had people from different parts of the world, which was great. We should reach out more to regions we didn't hear from this time.

**Community:** Breaks between talks matter a lot. People bonding at dinners or before the event helped too.

**Location:** Sofia worked out well. For the next one, a different location would help bring in new organizations and communities.
