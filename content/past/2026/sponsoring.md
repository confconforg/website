## Overview

How to fund conferences sustainably.

## Funding sources

- **Sponsorships** - Companies pay, get talk slots or booths
- **Tickets** - Some charge. Free/discounted for students and community.
- **Merchandise** - T-shirts, stickers. Covers costs, people like having them.
- **Catering revenue share** - Get percentage of what caterers make
- **Government/municipality** - Funding for venue and food
- **Community donations** - Open Collective, bank transfers, WordPress plugins
- **University partnerships** - Venue and help
- **Note:** Proprietary software companies usually run their own events instead

## Sponsorship packages

Usually offer:
- Booth space
- Website/material branding
- Talk slot (with approval)
- Catering sponsorship

Many have tiers for different company sizes and budgets. Some have low-cost ways for community projects to sponsor.

## Rejecting sponsors

You need policies and judgment.

**Reasons to say no:**
- Values don't align with open source
- Community backlash
- Problematic past behavior

**How to handle it:**
- Publish your policies
- Get community input on controversial sponsors
- Staff makes final call but often consults

## Other revenue ideas

- **Donor fundraising** - Community donations (navigate tax implications)
- **Premium experiences** - Expensive tickets for small speaker dinners
- **Certificates** - Valued in some regions
- **Volunteer awards** - Recognition and social events
- **Sponsorship tickets** - Company pays for community member attendance
- **Student org partnerships** - Help recruit and provide venue
- **Revenue share** - Catering gives you percentage

## What actually works

- **Relationships matter** - Knowing the right person at a company helps more than anything
- **Follow up** - Keep contact lists, reach out between events
- **Show value** - Make sure sponsors actually get something, not just logo placement
- **Different regions, different approaches** - What works varies
- **Build trust** - Publish policies, involve community on controversial decisions
- **Alignment** - Find companies that actually care about open source
