## Overview

Did the conference work? How do you know?

## Why check

- **What worked and what didn't** - Learn for next time.
- **Make it better** - Use what you learn.
- **Show sponsors and people** - Tell them what happened.
- **Help other organizers** - Share what you learned.

## What to track

**Numbers**
- How many people came
- Where they were from
- New vs. returning
- Different kinds of people
- What their roles are

**What happened**
- Did people go to sessions
- Were the topics interesting
- Did people talk to each other
- Did volunteers help

**Did it matter**
- Did people meet each other and stay in touch
- Did ideas turn into projects
- Did people actually use what they learned
- Is the FOSS community stronger for this

**Ask people**
- Quick survey after the event (2-3 questions max)
- Twitter and other social media
- Talk to sponsors
- Listen to people talking in the hallway

## How to collect feedback

- **Survey** - Google Forms is easy. Keep it short.
- **Social media** - Watch the hashtags on Twitter, Mastodon, etc.
- **Just listen** - Pay attention to what people say in real-time.
- **Follow up** - Email people weeks later. "Hey, did anything you learned actually get used?"

## After the event

- **Team meeting** - Get together soon and talk about what happened. While it's fresh.
- **Write it down** - What worked, what didn't, and why you made the decisions you made.
- **Share it** - Tell people what you learned. Other organizers will want to know.
- **Check on it later** - Look at your notes next year. Some of it will be old by then.

## Does it matter

- **Did people stay friends** - Are people still talking?
- **Did ideas become projects** - Did anything actually happen?
- **Is the community stronger** - Is FOSS organizing better because of this event?
