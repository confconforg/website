## Overview

How to pay for the conference.

## Ways to fund it

- **Free entry, sponsored** - Many FOSS conferences are free and sponsors pick up the tab.
- **Ask for donations** - Open Collective, bank transfers, WordPress plugins.
- **Merch** - T-shirts and stickers via Shopify + Printful. Not a money maker, but people like having them.
- **Grants** - Talk to foundations and community groups.

## Sponsors

- **Show them the value** - They want visibility, the chance to recruit people, and good community karma.
- **Different tiers** - Some sponsors have small budgets, some have big ones.
- **Share the work** - If multiple orgs co-host, you split the cost. That's the easiest way to make it sustainable.

## Food matters

Seriously, don't cheap out on food.

- **Dinner** - Pick good restaurants and locations. People remember good food.
- **Lunch** - Add fresh fruit. It's one thing people wish for.
- **Coffee & Tea** - Keep it flowing. Add snacks.
- **Breaks** - Take more of them than you think you need. That's when people actually talk.
