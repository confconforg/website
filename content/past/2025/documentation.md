## Overview

Getting new people up to speed and handing things over.

## Training someone new

- **Start small** - Give them easy stuff at first.
- **Work together** - Pair them with someone experienced.
- **Don't leave before you train a replacement** - Seriously.
- **Build them up** - Give them bigger tasks as they get confident.

## Writing things down

- **Don't over-document** - Too many unclear docs is worse than no docs.
- **Document why, not just what** - Future organizers need to know why decisions were made, not just that they were.
- **Use simple tools** - Something mobile-friendly and easy to update. Something you'll actually use.
- **Write after the event** - While it's fresh. Post-mortems help.

## Sharing knowledge

- **Different places** - Docs, chat, email. Knowledge doesn't have to all be in one place.
- **Update things** - Docs get old. Refresh them sometimes.
- **Show your thinking** - When you write down decisions, explain what you were trying to do and what the constraints were.

## Real challenges

- Too much documentation is overwhelming for new people.
- Some people learn by doing, not reading.
- You need some process but not so much that it kills creativity.
