## Overview

Getting started organizing a conference.

## First things

- **Know why** - Why are you doing this? What do you want to happen?
- **Get help** - Find people who share your vision. You can't do this solo.
- **Set the tone** - What kind of community do you want? What behavior fits?

## Basics that work

- **Unconference style** - Structure should help people participate, not stop them.
- **Reach everyone** - Actively invite people from different places and backgrounds.
- **Breaks matter** - People learn from talking to each other as much as from talks.
- **Short talks** - Boring talks kill the vibe.
- **Plan but stay flexible** - Have a schedule but leave room for stuff to happen.

## Big decisions

- **Where** - Pick a place where people can hang out. Where they can explore a bit.
- **How long** - 1 day? 2 days? Extra time with no scheduled talks helps mingling.
- **Free or pay** - Free is easier for people to come to. Costs more on your side.
- **Language** - English-only or multiple languages? Affects who can come and how much work.
- **Who's it for** - Experienced people? Newcomers? Mix?

## Do

- Plan ahead
- Write down what you learn
- Think about what went wrong after
- Ask for help
- Start simple

## Don't

- Over-plan. Leave room for people to make things happen.
- Assume people won't help. They usually will.
- Make food cheap. People remember food.
- Try to do this alone.
- Wait until the last minute to figure out speakers.
