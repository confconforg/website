## Overview

Codes of conduct and what to do when stuff goes wrong.

## Why have one

- **Sets the bar** - People know what's acceptable and what's not.
- **Prevents things from escalating** - Small problems stay small if everyone knows the rules.
- **Makes newcomers feel safe** - They know the space is watched out for.
- **Helps with hard decisions** - If you need to remove someone, documented standards make it fair.

## What to put in it

- **Be welcoming** - Say that different backgrounds and ideas are good.
- **What good looks like** - Describe respectful participation.
- **How to report a problem** - Who do people tell? How?
- **What happens** - If someone breaks the rules, what's the consequence?
- **Appeal** - Can someone argue if they think it's unfair?

## When things go wrong

**Different kinds of problems:**

- **Abuse** - Someone's being directly hostile or harassing. Act fast.
- **Toxic patterns** - Someone blocks progress, talks down to others, takes themselves way too seriously.
- **Arguments** - People disagree but both want to work it out.

**How to handle it:**

1. Listen to what happened.
2. Talk to everyone involved.
3. Figure out what happened and what to do.
4. Explain your decision clearly.
5. Follow up and make sure it's actually fixed.

**Key things:**

- Let people improve if they can.
- Be clear about what you expect and what happens if they don't follow it.
- Don't kick someone out immediately unless they crossed a hard line.
- Make the call fast once you decide.
- Don't let one bad person turn others toxic.

## Hard situations

**Someone's blocking everything:**
- Having written rules helps you make decisions even if they won't cooperate.
- Sometimes you have to remove them.

**People disagree about what to do:**
- Usually it's not about values, just about how to do things. Talk it out.
- If the actual goals are different, the organization needs to pick a direction.

**Leadership stuff:**
- Set the right tone from the top. People copy what leadership does.
- If someone's acting against the culture, deal with it fast.
