ConfConf uses the *[unconference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unconference)*
format, where participants provide most of the content themselves and there is
only a minimal programme.

To help kickstart the event, we'll start with a round of introductions so
everyone can meet their fellow participants and the organisations they
represent.

Each day opens with a short remark, but beyond that, the content comes from the
participants.

At the start of each day, participants write topics on a flipchart (which can
be added to throughout the day). Periodically, everyone gathers in the main
room where up to four discussion groups are formed, then breaks out into the
discussion rooms.

Finally, at the end of each day, we close with a group *wrap-up* session, where
everyone is invited to share key takeaways.

## Topics

To spark ideas, here are some possible discussion topics:

  * **Shaping the programme** – How do you select content? Do you set themes or let them emerge? Do you scout speakers or wait for submissions?
  * **Growing your audience** – How do you attract attendees and reach underrepresented groups? How do you manage volunteers and enforce a code of conduct?
  * **Funding & ethics** – How do you raise money through donations, sponsorships, or merchandise while keeping things transparent? How much say should sponsors have?
  * **Tech & logistics** – What tools and infrastructure do you rely on? How do you handle video, streaming, and on-site operations?

These are just suggestions, any topic that fits the *Conference for Conference Organisers* theme is welcome.

## Timing

On both days, the conference rooms are open from 09:00 onward, with the start of
the conference scheduled for 09:30. Wrap-up is scheduled for 17:30.

Don't despair, discussions can still continue over [dinner](/practical#food).
