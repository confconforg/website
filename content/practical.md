## Getting there

Thailand

The campus is large, so allow some extra time to walk from the gate to the
venue. More details on the exact building will follow.

### Airplane

Bangkok has two airports:

- **Suvarnabhumi (BKK)**, about 40 km from the venue, is served by all major
  international and regional [airlines](https://suvarnabhumi.airportthai.co.th/).
  Take the Airport Rail Link to Phaya Thai (around 30 minutes), then change to
  the BTS (see below).
- **Don Mueang (DMK)**, about 18 km from the venue, is mostly used by low-cost
  carriers. The SRT Red Line takes you from the airport to Bang Khen station in
  around 10 minutes (see below).

### BTS Skytrain

The closest station is **Kasetsart University (N13)** on the BTS Sukhumvit
line (light green). From the city centre, take any northbound Sukhumvit line
train (towards Khu Khot); from Phaya Thai (N2) the ride takes around 20
minutes.

From the station, it's a short walk to the campus. Motorbike taxis and regular
taxis are also available for the last stretch.

Single-journey tickets can be bought from the machines or ticket office at
each station.

### Train (SRT Red Line)

**Bang Khen (RN04)** station on the SRT Dark Red Line is about 1 km from the
venue, a walk of around 15 minutes, or a short taxi or motorbike taxi ride.

- **From Don Mueang Airport:** take the Red Line towards Krung Thep Aphiwat;
  Bang Khen is four stops away (around 10 minutes).
- **From the city centre:** take the MRT Blue Line to Bang Sue and transfer to
  the Red Line at Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal, then ride three stops
  towards Rangsit.

### Grab or taxi

[Grab](https://www.grab.com/th/en/) is the easiest way to get around by car:
the price is fixed up front and you can enter the address above as the
destination.

Regular metered taxis are plentiful and cheap. Always make sure the driver
switches on the meter; if they refuse or quote a fixed price, just take the
next one. It helps to show the driver the address in Thai:
**มหาวิทยาลัยเกษตรศาสตร์ บางเขน** (Kasetsart University, Bangkhen).

From the airports, use the official taxi queue (Suvarnabhumi: level 1;
Don Mueang: arrivals level). Expect to pay the meter plus a 50 baht airport
surcharge and any expressway tolls. Depending on traffic, the ride from
Suvarnabhumi takes 45 to 90 minutes.
