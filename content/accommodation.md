We have negotiated a special group rate at [KU Home Hotel](https://www.ku-home.ku.ac.th), located on the Kasetsart University campus just a few steps from the venue.

## Hotel details

- **Website:** [KU Home Hotel](https://www.ku-home.ku.ac.th)
- **Rate:** 750 THB per night
- **Address:** KU Home, 50 Ngamwongwan Rd, Lat Yao, Chatuchak, Bangkok 10900, Thailand
- **Room types:** Twin (2 single beds) or Double (1 large bed), subject to availability
- **Breakfast:** Not included. The hotel offers breakfast for an extra charge. For your convenience, the ConfConf organizers will arrange breakfast at the venue each conference day.

## How to book

Reserve your room together with your ticket on our [Tickets page](/tickets/). Select **"Hotel reservation – pay at check-in"** and tell us your arrival and departure dates. We'll check availability with the hotel and confirm your booking by email.

Rooms are limited, so please book early.

## Payment

No online payment. You pay at hotel check-in, in **CASH only** (Thai Baht, THB).

## Cancellation

Once confirmed, your reservation cannot be changed or refunded. The organizers have guaranteed these rooms with the hotel, so we would need to cover the cost of any no-shows.

If you can't make it, please tell us as early as possible at [info@confconf.org](mailto:info@confconf.org), so we can pass the room to another attendee. Thank you for your understanding!
