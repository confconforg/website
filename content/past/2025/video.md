## Overview

Recording and streaming talks.

## Tools

- **OBS** - Free, works great. Stream to YouTube or wherever.
- **Venueless** - Lets remote people watch and participate alongside in-person folks.
- **Jitsi** - Free video calls, good for Q&A.

## Think about

**Streaming**
- Live streaming lets remote people join.
- Takes bandwidth and someone needs to babysit it.
- Having videos archived is worth doing.

**Quality**
- 1080p is fine.
- Watch your bandwidth and storage.

**Where to put videos**
- YouTube, PeerTube, or your own server.
- Add transcripts so people can read along.
- Some conferences sell early access, then release for free later.

**Permission**
- Ask people before recording them.
- If someone says no recording, don't record their session.
- Don't show faces of people in the background who didn't agree.

## In practice

- **Get someone on it** - Have one person in charge of video setup and streaming.
- **Test first** - Test everything before you go live. Audio always breaks.
- **Have backups** - Backup microphone, backup cable, backup streaming link.
- **Decide who edits** - Before the event, figure out who'll edit, caption, and upload.

## Remote people

- **Video rooms** - Let people at home join breakout sessions.
- **Chat** - Some platforms let in-person and remote people chat together.
- **Everyone watches** - Record everything. People can watch later if they missed it.
